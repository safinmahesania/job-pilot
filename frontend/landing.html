HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, then it runs itself

You set the rules once. From there, JobPilot reads every new posting so you don’t have to.

Step one

Tell it what you want

Your locations, the levels you’re after, a salary floor, and the keywords to skip. Change any of it whenever your search shifts — nothing is set in stone.

Step two

It reads and scores

Across 30+ sources, every new job is filtered against your rules, then scored from 0 to 100 — with a short reason you can read, trust, or push back on.

Step three

You apply to the good ones

Open a ranked feed, generate a resume and cover letter matched to the posting, and let the extension fill the application. You review and submit.

WHAT YOU GET

Built to cut the noise, not add to it

Every feature answers one question: is this job worth your next hour?

Scored, with a reason

Each match shows why it scored what it did — so you can trust the number, or disagree with it and move on.

Your rules, enforced

Only jobs that fit your constraints surface. Set nothing and everything comes through — you decide how tight the filter is.

One feed, only yours

A shared job pool, a private feed. What you’ve seen, saved, applied to, or dismissed is tracked per job, for you alone.

Tailored in a click

Generate a resume and cover letter matched to the posting — kept with the job, so you always have the copy you sent.

Autofill the application

The Chrome extension maps your saved answers onto the form. You review every field — it never invents an answer.

Private by design

Your name, email, phone and address never go into an AI prompt. The model writes around placeholders; JobPilot fills them in.

YOUR DETAILS STAY YOURS

The tool reads jobs. It doesn’t hand out your life.

Scoring and writing need context about the work you’ve done — not who you are. So that’s the line JobPilot draws, and keeps.

  • Contact details are never placed in a prompt — the model works around placeholders.
  • Voluntary questions on a form are left blank for you to answer, never guessed.
  • If your profile doesn’t answer something, the field stays empty. A guess on an application is a liability.
