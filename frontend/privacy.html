This Privacy Policy explains what information JobPilot ("we", "us") collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using JobPilot you agree to this policy.

Who we are

JobPilot is operated by [Legal entity / owner name], based in [City, Province/Country]. For any privacy question or request, contact [contact email].

Information we collect

  • Account information — your email address and login credentials, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). If you sign in with Google, we receive your email and name from Google.
  • Profile information you provide — job titles, locations, seniority, skills, work experience, education, salary preferences, and any other details you enter to power job matching.
  • Application content — answers you save to application questions, and résumés/cover letters generated for you.
  • Usage data — which jobs you view, save, dismiss or mark as applied, so we can rank future matches.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) used for security and rate-limiting.

How we use your information

  • To match, score and rank job postings against your profile.
  • To generate tailored résumés and cover letters at your request.
  • To operate, secure and improve the service (including preventing abuse).
  • To communicate with you about your account or the service.

Third parties who process your data

To deliver the service, we share limited data with the following processors:

  • Supabase — database and authentication (stores your account, profile and job data).
  • AI model providers (Google Gemini, Cerebras) — when we score jobs or generate résumés/cover letters, the relevant profile and job text is sent to these providers to produce the result. Do not enter information you are not comfortable processing this way.
  • Job data sources — job listings are fetched from third-party job boards and search APIs (for example Adzuna, and various company career sites). We do not send them your personal data.
  • Hosting/infrastructure providers — to run the application.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not show third-party advertising.

Data retention

We keep your account and profile data for as long as your account is active. Shared job listings are automatically removed after a set period once they are no longer relevant. When you delete your account, your personal data (profile, saved/applied jobs, generated materials, application answers, notifications and settings) is deleted.

Your rights

  • Access & export — you can export your job data from within the app.
  • Deletion — you can permanently delete your account and associated personal data from the app settings at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as PIPEDA (Canada) or the GDPR (EU).
  • Correction — you can edit your profile information at any time.

To exercise any right you cannot complete in-app, contact [contact email].

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, isolation between user accounts) to protect your data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to keep your information safe and will notify you of a material breach as required by law.

Children

JobPilot is not directed to anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; we will change the "last updated" date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
